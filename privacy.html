Primary Science Diagnostic – Privacy Policy Primary Science Diagnostic is an educational tool designed to help teachers create and analyse Primary Science diagnostic assessments. Google account access The application uses Google OAuth to access Google Drive for the purpose of creating Google Forms and storing application data and generated files. Google account access is authorised only by the application administrator. Other users of the application do not sign in using their Google accounts. Data The application may process assessment-related information entered by teachers or submitted through diagnostic forms. Data is stored in the Google Drive account authorised by the application administrator. The application does not sell personal information or use Google user data for advertising. Third-party services The application uses Google services including Google Drive and Google Forms to provide its functionality. Contact Questions regarding this privacy policy may be directed to the application administrator. Last updated: September 2026.
