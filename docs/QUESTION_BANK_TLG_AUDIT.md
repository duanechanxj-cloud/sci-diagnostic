## What the analysis found

### 1. Coverage by LO was complete, but some Practice LOs were thin
The original 326-question bank covered all 82 assessment-focused LOs, but **19 Practice LOs had only 3 questions each**. This is not a missing-LO problem; it is an evidence-depth problem. One fresh parallel item was added to every one of those 19 Practice LOs, so every Practice LO now has at least 4 questions.

### 2. Several high-value TLG misconceptions were not explicitly probed
Targeted questions were added for documented ideas such as: living vs non-living based on more than one characteristic; different insect life cycles; magnet size vs strength; plant food/water misconceptions; digestion beginning in the mouth; shadows not being matter; blankets not being heat sources; 'coldness' not flowing; offspring receiving information from both parents; evaporation vs boiling; open-circuit current; parallel-bulb brightness; force acting without contact; and force vs energy.

### 3. Two P5 cell questions exceeded the intended primary assessment scope
The Reproduction TLG states that pupils need to appreciate that a cell is a basic unit of life and are **not required to know specific cell parts and functions**. Two existing questions about chloroplasts and shared plant/animal cell structures were therefore replaced, retaining their stable Question_IDs and increasing their Question_Version to 3.

### 4. Some already-strong topics did not need artificial padding
The goal was not to force every LO to the same count. Broad topics that already had sufficient independent probes were left alone unless a specific TLG gap was identified. This keeps the library diagnostic rather than bloated.

## Scope / structural checks

- No Values/Ethics/Attitudes LO_IDs.
- No duplicate Question_IDs.
- No exact duplicate question stems.
- All four options are present and distinct in every question.
- All Correct_Option values are A/B/C/D.
- All LO_IDs exist in the 82-row assessment-focused learning-outcome reference.
- Level and Topic_Code match each LO_ID.
- No P3 Life Cycles germination/pollination/fertilisation/seed-dispersal scope creep.
- No P3 Materials density or formal transparent/translucent/opaque terminology.
- No xylem/phloem terminology in plant transport.
- No alveoli/artery/vein/capillary detail in P5 Human Systems.
- No voltage/resistance/potential-difference/electron terminology in P5 Electrical System.
- No air-resistance/water-resistance terminology in P6 Forces.
